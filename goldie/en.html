Goldie’s Big Adventure

A cheerful platformer for iPhone: jump, sniff and dig your way through 50 levels with Goldie – or your own dog – and rescue lost puppies.

Coming soon to theApp Store

A look at Goldie

Five worlds, each with its own landscape, enemies and music.

What awaits you

A game to laugh and cheer along with – for young and old.

🐕

Your very own dog

Design your dog with coat colour, ears, tail and name – or let a photo suggest the colours.

🗺️

50 levels in 5 worlds

Park, beach, enchanted forest, snowy mountains and the big city, each with its own music.

👑

5 very different bosses

From the vacuum robot to the street sweeper: every boss needs a different tactic.

🐾

Sniff, dig and fetch

Plus swimming, zoomies and relaxing puzzle levels in between.

📅

Something new every day

Daily walk, weekly challenge and 12 seasonal events throughout the year.

🏡

Garden and puppy album

Rescue a lost puppy in every level and decorate your garden.

🌍

Four languages

English, German, French and Italian.

💛

Fair and fully playable

The whole game can be finished without buying anything. Purchases are protected by a parental check.

🐶 “Help!” – In every level a lost puppy is waiting somewhere. Find it, and it follows you all the way home and moves into your garden.
