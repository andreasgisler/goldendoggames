A cheerful platformer for iPhone and iPad: jump, sniff and dig your way through 200 levels with Goldie – or your own dog – and rescue lost puppies.

A look at Goldie

Ten worlds, each with its own landscape, enemies and music.

What awaits you

A game to laugh and cheer along with – for young and old.

🎁

Power-ups and extras

Shield, extra heart, magnet and super nose help in tricky levels. Plus a daily reminder for your walk.

☁️

Progress in iCloud

Your progress is safely stored – even on a new iPhone or iPad.

🐕

Your very own dog

Design your dog with coat colour, ears, tail and name – or let a photo suggest the colours.

🗺️

200 levels in 10 worlds

From the park through crystal caves, desert and jungle to Cloudland and Toyland, each with its own music.

👑

10 bosses

From the vacuum robot to Robo Rumble: every boss needs the right tactic.

🐾

Sniff, dig and fetch

Plus swimming, zoomies and relaxing puzzle levels in between.

📅

Something new every day

Daily walk, weekly challenge and 12 seasonal events throughout the year.

🏡

Garden and puppy album

Rescue a lost puppy in every level and decorate your garden.

🌍

Four languages

English, German, French and Italian.

💛

Fair and fully playable

The whole game can be finished without buying anything. Purchases are purely optional extras.

“Help!” – In every level a lost puppy is waiting somewhere. Find it, and it follows you all the way home and moves into your garden.
