Take a look

Two buttons: speed up on the left, jump on the right. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

What awaits you

A Christmas game for the whole family, full of little surprises.

🌍

90 levels, 9 regions

Snow Village, Pine Forest, Swiss Alps, Ice Peaks, City of Lights, Lantern Festival, Summer Beach, North Pole and a bonus New Year’s Eve. Each region has its own scenery and its own twist.

📅

A real Advent calendar

24 doors, and in December each one opens on its day. Behind door 24 awaits the grand finale against the blizzard.

🎅

8 characters, lots of outfits

Santa, Mrs. Claus, an elf, Samichlaus, Schmutzli, a snowman, a polar bear and Goldie. Plus hats, scarves, glasses and Christmas jumpers.

🦌

Reindeer and variety

Train reindeer with special skills, race a cheeky goblin, escape avalanches and a yeti, and defeat the Frost King.

✉️

90 wish letters

Every level hides a wish letter from a child somewhere in the world. Together they make a heart-warming album.

💛

Fair and fully playable

The whole game can be finished without buying anything. Ads can be switched off. In 10 languages.
