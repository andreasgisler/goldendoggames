Privacy Policy

In short: the game needs no account and doesn’t track you. It shows non-personalized ads, which you can turn off with “No ads”. Data is only processed for ads and purchases.

1. Who is responsible

Andreas Gisler (Golden Dog Games), Schachenweidstrasse 31, 6030 Ebikon, Switzerland, support@goldendoggames.com

2. Data on your device

Your progress, coins, stars, achievements and settings (language, music, sounds) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data as well.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; Apple’s or Google’s privacy policies apply to them. To verify purchases and credit your coins, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). This involves a randomly generated, anonymous user ID and purchase details (product, time, receipt). The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on [the safeguards stated by RevenueCat – please verify]. More information: RevenueCat’s privacy policy revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Advertising

The game shows ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). We only request non-personalized ads and do not use app tracking. To deliver ads, Google still processes technical data such as IP address, device type, operating system, app version and information about the ads shown. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first via Google’s consent dialog (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can change it at any time in the settings under “Ad privacy”. Buying “No ads” removes the ads between puzzles; optional reward videos remain available. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Retention

Purchase data is kept only as long as needed to process purchases and meet legal retention requirements (in Switzerland generally up to 10 years under Art. 958f of the Swiss Code of Obligations).

[Choose one: The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. / The game is suitable for children; purchases are protected by a parental gate.] Parents can also block in-app purchases in the device settings.

7. Your rights

For users in Switzerland the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) applies; for users in the EU the GDPR applies as well. You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Requests to support@goldendoggames.com.

8. Changes

We will update this policy if the game changes. Last updated: 17 October 2026
